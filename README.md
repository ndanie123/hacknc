# Project Overview
For Project Log Companion, we created a website that prompts the users for any negative symptoms effecting there day to day life (what the symptoms are, when they came about, and things that happened that may have caused it. Afterwards, this information, through a Gemini API key, the responses
are converted to structured output, which is then packaged into a pdf which can be sent to the patient's provider ahead of their appointment in order to provide context and speed up time to diagnose the issue and provide proper medical advice

# The Why
When patients schedule appointments with their doctors, they often have to backtrack weeks to remember what caused their symptoms and specifically when they occurred. By having an organized form of their information beforehand, doctors spend less time nailing down the cause and duration of the symptoms and more time on fast tracking solutions that can help the patient and make better use of the appointment time.
